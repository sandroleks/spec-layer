Frequently asked questions.

Get support
Do I need Pro to generate documentation?

No. Component specs, foundation docs, source-change tracking, and Copy for AI are free. Only optional AI writing has a free usage allowance. Pro adds library publishing, more AI writing, and priority support.

What uses my AI writing allowance?

One successful AI writing response counts as one use. Failed requests do not count. Repeating an unchanged request within the 24-hour cache window does not count as another use. Measurements, states, anatomy, tokens, and Copy for AI do not use your allowance.

Does my design data leave Figma?

Component extraction, canvas rendering, and Copy for AI run inside Figma. When you enable AI writing, a structured summary and, when available, a component image are sent through Spec Layer to Anthropic. When you publish, Spec Layer stores the library bundle so developers with its pull key can fetch it. Read the privacy policy for the full data flow.

Does the CLI generate my UI?

No. It delivers structured component and token context to your repository. Your developers or coding agent use that context when implementing the interface.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout, your license key arrives by email. Open License in the Figma plugin, paste the key, and select Activate. One key covers your individual use across files. You can manage your subscription through Lemon Squeezy.

Can I get a refund?

If Pro doesn’t work out, contact support within 30 days. See the refund policy for details.
