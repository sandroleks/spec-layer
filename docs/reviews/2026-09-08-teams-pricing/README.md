# Teams pricing proposal — 8 September 2026

Added a third pricing card to the local website draft. The card shows **Teams / Custom**, with a **Discuss your team’s needs** email link to the existing support contact and a prefilled subject. It offers a discussion of team licensing, agreed file/usage limits, onboarding, integration scoping, and support. It does not advertise an existing shared workspace, SSO, team ownership, or a service-level guarantee.

The monthly/yearly toggle still changes only Pro. Teams pricing and scope are agreed separately. Three cards appear side by side above 1100px; the Teams card spans the second row on tablets, and all three stack on mobile.

## Suggested commercial starting point

**From $99 per team per month**, initially scoped around up to five named publishers, an agreed usage allowance, and guided setup. This is a proposal, not a price applied to checkout or published in the card. Quote bespoke integration development and ongoing custom maintenance separately after understanding the requirements.

Five current monthly Pro subscriptions cost $39.95. A $99 minimum leaves $59.05 per month above that baseline for the additional service, but this is not a validated margin estimate: real support time and AI usage need tracking. Avoid bundling open-ended development or unlimited team AI usage into that price. Licensing can be arranged for named users; the existing individual Pro key must not be shared.

For category context, [Supernova's public pricing](https://www.supernova.io/pricing) currently shows $35 per full seat per month on Pro and custom Enterprise pricing. It covers a broader feature set, so this is a positioning reference rather than a direct comparison or proof of customers' willingness to pay. The proposed $99 is primarily anchored to Spec Layer's own Pro price and a limited service scope. Validate the package with the first team enquiries before fixing annual terms.
