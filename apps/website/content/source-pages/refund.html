You can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase, for any reason. This applies to your initial purchase and to each renewal charge within its own 30-day window.

Email oleksandr.kurchev@gmail.com from the address you used to buy, or include your Lemon Squeezy order number. We will confirm and process the refund. When a refund is issued, the associated Pro license key is deactivated and Pro features stop working. You can keep using the free tier.

The free tier
