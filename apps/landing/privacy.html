Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

This policy explains what data the Spec Layer Figma plugin handles and why. Spec Layer is provided by Oleksandr Kurchev (the "Provider", "we", "us"), an individual acting as the data controller. You can reach us at oleksandr.kurchev@gmail.com.

Our starting point is to collect as little as possible. The plugin has no user accounts, and everything deterministic (measurements, states, anatomy, tokens) runs locally in Figma without sending your data anywhere.

What we collect

  • Component content you choose to document. When you use an AI-written documentation feature, a derived summary of the component you select (such as layer names, text, and structure) and, when it fits the size limits, a rendered image of that component are sent to our proxy and on to our AI provider. Foundation group writing sends the selected token names and resolved values, but no component image. This happens only when you ask for AI writing.
  • License, identity, and usage data. The free tier uses your Figma user identifier to maintain a quota; our backend stores it only as a salted SHA-256 digest. If you activate Pro, the plugin stores the license key in Figma's local plugin storage and our proxy sends it to Lemon Squeezy for validation. Our backend uses unsalted SHA-256 digests, not raw license keys, for cache keys, quota identities, and logs. We also process generation counts and timestamps to apply free-tier limits and Pro fair use. We do not build a profile of your designs.
  • Order information. When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy shares limited order details with us (such as your email and order identifier) so we can issue and manage your license key.

We do not collect payment card details. Those are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy.

How we use it

  • to generate the documentation you ask for;
  • to validate your license and apply your plan's usage allowance;
  • to detect and prevent abuse and enforce fair use;
  • to provide support and process refunds.

Our legal bases are performance of our contract with you (providing the plugin and Pro features) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

AI processing

AI-written documentation is generated by Anthropic using its Claude models. The content you submit is sent to Anthropic through our proxy solely to produce your documentation. Anthropic says commercial API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default and are normally deleted from its backend within 30 days, subject to its stated legal, safety, and contractual exceptions. To avoid repeated processing, our proxy may cache a generated response for up to 24 hours under a content-derived key; it does not cache the submitted prompt or image.

Service providers (sub-processors)

  • Anthropic — AI generation of documentation.
  • Cloudflare — hosting of our proxy and license infrastructure.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments and subscription management (merchant of record).
  • Figma — the environment the plugin runs in.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms. Some may process data outside your country; where that happens, appropriate safeguards apply.

Data retention

We keep license and usage records for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal and accounting obligations. Our proxy does not persist submitted component summaries, rendered images, or token values. It may retain the generated response for up to 24 hours for idempotent retries. Anthropic's standard API retention is described in the AI processing section above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you can request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, and request a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email oleksandr.kurchev@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Cookies
