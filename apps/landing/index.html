Questions

How does the license work?
Buy Pro and your license key arrives by email. Open the plugin in Figma, go to Settings, paste the key, and you're on Pro. One key covers your individual use across your files.
What counts as an AI generation?
One successful AI call for a component. Regenerating docs for an unchanged component hits the cache and costs nothing. Failed calls never count against your quota. Everything deterministic (measurements, states, anatomy, tokens) is free and doesn't use generations at all.
Can I get a refund?
If Pro doesn't work out, email alex@neuronux.com and we'll sort out a refund.
Is Pro really unlimited?
For normal individual use, yes. Documenting your whole library in one sitting is fine. Safeguards only kick in on automated or shared usage patterns, and we'll always contact you before limiting anything.
